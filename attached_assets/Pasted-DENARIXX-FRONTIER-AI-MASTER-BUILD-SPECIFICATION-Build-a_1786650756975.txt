DENARIXX FRONTIER AI — MASTER BUILD SPECIFICATION

Build a serious, production-oriented AI research and model-engineering system for:

Company: Denarixx Digital Solutions
Research Organization: Denarixx AI Research
Platform: Denarixx Frontier AI
Initial Research Program: Denarixx D0
Future Foundation Model: Denarixx D1

This must NOT be a chatbot clone, OpenAI wrapper, fake AI dashboard, benchmark mockup, or generic SaaS application.

The long-term research objective is exceptionally ambitious:

Develop Denarixx-owned foundation-model technology capable of eventually competing with and, where objectively demonstrated, surpassing leading frontier AI systems across reasoning, mathematics, coding, science, factuality, multilingual intelligence, multimodal understanding, long-context tasks, agents, tool use, memory, reliability, latency, cost efficiency and computational efficiency.

Do NOT claim that this objective has already been achieved.

Every performance claim must eventually be demonstrated through reproducible evaluations.

The immediate objective is different:

Build the real engineering and experimentation foundation required for Denarixx to independently create, train, evaluate, serve and improve its own neural language models.
